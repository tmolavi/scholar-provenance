## How to Submit Your Research

Participation in the community showcase is 100% voluntary and opt-in:

1. In your project workspace, run:
   ```bash
   scholar-provenance showcase
   ```
2. Open a new issue using the [Showcase Submission Template](https://github.com/tmolavi/scholar-provenance/issues/new?template=showcase.md) and paste your generated JSON.
3. Once reviewed for basic scholarly integrity, your project will be linked in this table!
