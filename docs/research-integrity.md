# Research Integrity & Ethical Publication

ScholarProvenance exists to empower legitimate researchers to document real work rigorously. It is engineered with architectural guardrails against academic fraud and "paper mill" automation.

## Core Ethical Standards

### 1. Human Authorship Exclusivity
- The AI agent or underlying LLM is never credited as a paper author.
- Author entries require explicit human designation.
- Affiliations, academic degrees, and institutions are never hallucinated.

### 2. Mandatory AI Assistance Disclosure
In accordance with COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics) and major publisher policies (IEEE, ACM, Elsevier, Springer Nature), every manuscript generated with ScholarProvenance includes an explicit disclosure block:

> *"The authors disclose that an AI research agent running ScholarProvenance was utilized to assist in literature discovery, evidence ledger formatting, and citation verification. All empirical experiments were conducted by the authors, and all analytical claims and final prose were reviewed, verified, and approved by the human authors."*

### 3. Data Integrity & Negative Findings
- Raw data must never be altered or smoothed to fit hypotheses.
- Contradictory evidence, edge-case failures, and architectural overhead must be explicitly documented in dedicated sections.
