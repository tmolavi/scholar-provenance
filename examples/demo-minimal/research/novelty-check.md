# Novelty Check & Prior Art Verification

## Prior Art Scrutiny
- A search across OpenAlex and arXiv confirms that while generic retrieval augmentation is heavily studied, explicit in-memory evidence ledger architectures with runtime taxonomy gates remain relatively unbenchmarked in software engineering literature.
- We temper all claims: We do not claim to be "the first" to evaluate retrieval architectures; rather, our evidence suggests that in-memory taxonomy-enforced ledgers yield significant throughput benefits in controlled local concurrency environments.
